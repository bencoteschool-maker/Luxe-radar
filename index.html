A smarter trend read

What makes a product trend?

For a trustworthy “most wanted” list, check multiple signals instead of treating a social post or a single bestseller list as proof.

01

Official bestsellers

Review brand bestseller pages and new arrivals. Record the date, country and exact product name so the list can be refreshed consistently.

02

Search interest

Compare search interest over time in Canada and the USA. Search popularity is a signal of attention, not proof of purchases.

03

Social conversation

Watch repeated mentions across different creators and platforms. Separate sponsored promotion from independent discussion.

Free research starting points: Google Trends, and official brand stores linked on each product. This site does not scrape websites or claim real-time data.
